Where the data goes

Readings and predictions are stored on this Pi. When MQTT is enabled, readings pass through the MQTT broker on this Pi to a connected PC. This lab connection uses port 1883 without transport encryption. No cloud service is required.

Do not use these readings to identify people or infer attendance. Review your retention setting and account for archives, exports and deletion. Changing an ID does not necessarily anonymise data.
